Why it exists

Every email carries your brand

Your signature is the one part of every email you send that never changes. A clear one tells people who you are and how to reach you, and it looks the same in every inbox. Email Signature builds one in a few minutes, with no account and no design tools.

What it makes

A signature that pastes straight in

The result is HTML built the way mail apps expect: tables and inline styles, so it holds together in Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail and Yahoo Mail.
