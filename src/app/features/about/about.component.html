A professional email signature in minutes.

Email Signature Builder is a free tool that turns your name, title, company, and logo into a polished HTML signature — pick a template, fill in your details, and copy or download it for the email client you use.

What it is

Five templates, one form.

Fill in your name, title, company, contact details, and an optional logo and call-to-action link. Choose from Clean, Centered, Sidebar, Minimal, or Spotlight, and preview the result live.

Category

Email signature generator.

A focused utility, not a full brand-kit tool — one form, one preview, one HTML output, ready to paste into your email client's signature settings.

Who made it

Where it works

Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, Yahoo Mail.
